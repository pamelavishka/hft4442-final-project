Virtual Hotel AI Robot: Designing and Developing a Conversational AI Agent

1. Overview

The final project of HFT 4442/HMG 6440 provides students with an opportunity to apply the concepts and technologies learned in this course to the development of a conversational AI agent for a hypothetical hotel.

For this project, you will be provided with information about a hypothetical hotel, including its rooms, amenities, restaurants, services, policies, facilities, and other relevant information. Your task is to transform this information into a knowledge base and use it to develop a conversational AI chatbot that can interact with hotel guests.

The chatbot should have a virtual appearance (e.g., an AI avatar or virtual hotel agent) and should be designed to provide realistic guest-service experience.

The purpose of this project is not simply to "make a chatbot." You are expected to consider the technical, psychological, and management implications of using conversational AI in a hospitality environment.

This project should be completed in a group of 4–6 students.

The final project consists of four stages:

Submit a project plan

Develop the Virtual Hotel AI Agent 

Market Examination

Present and demonstrate the AI Agent

3.3. Psychological Considerations

The purpose of this section is to examine the AI agent from the guest's perspective.

Human-AI Interaction

Discuss how guests are expected to interact with your AI agent.

Should the AI behave like a human employee? 

Should the AI explicitly identify itself as AI? 

What personality should the AI have? 

How formal or informal should the AI be? 

How should the AI respond to different types of guests? 

Emotional and Difficult Interactions

Your chatbot should be tested with emotionally difficult situations.

For example:

"My room is disgusting. I want to speak to a manager."

"This is the worst hotel experience I've ever had."

"I missed my flight and I'm extremely frustrated. Can you help me?"

Discuss how your AI should respond to emotional guests and explain when AI should transfer the interaction to a human employee.

A major objective of this project is to understand that AI should not necessarily handle every customer interaction. Your team should develop a clear strategy for determining when a guest request should be handled by AI and when it should be transferred to a human employee.

Human Handoff/ Escalation Strategy

Identify the situations that should trigger human intervention. These may include:

The AI does not know the answer. 

The guest makes a serious complaint. 

The guest requests compensation.

Lastly, the team should provide systematic performance measures to evaluate the performance of the AI agent in daily operations. 

Step 3 – Market Examination (5 points)

The third stage of the project is the Market Examination. Each team will test its Virtual Hotel AI Agent from the customer's perspective. 

Each team will create a 60-second video demonstrating how its Virtual Hotel AI Agent interacts with a hotel guest. The videos will then be evaluated through a customer perception survey. The final Market Examination score will be based on overall customer satisfaction. 

The purpose of this activity is to answer a fundamental question:

Would customers actually want to interact with your AI agent?

The video should show:

A hotel guest interacting with the AI agent

The AI agent's virtual appearance

The AI agent's personality and communication style

A realistic guest-service interaction

How the AI agent responds to the guest's needs

The video should be designed mimicking real customer-AI interaction. 

For example, the video could show a guest:

Asking about hotel amenities

Requesting a restaurant recommendation

Asking for assistance during a stay

Seeking local recommendations

Reporting a problem

Asking multiple related questions

Requesting assistance that requires human intervention

Survey participants will watch the videos and evaluate each hotel's AI agent based on their perceived guest experience.

The primary evaluation measure will be overall customer satisfaction, measured on a 1–5 scale:

1 = Very Dissatisfied
2 = Dissatisfied
3 = Neutral
4 = Satisfied
5 = Very Satisfied

The survey may also include additional questions about participants' perceptions of the AI agent, such as:

Helpfulness

Friendliness

Competence

Trustworthiness

Anthropomorphism

Appropriateness for hotel service

Willingness to interact with the AI agent

The Market Examination will account for 5 points of the final project grade.

The Customer Satisfaction score will be the average satisfaction rating received by the team's AI agent.
